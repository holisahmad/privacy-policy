Summary: Jadwal Sholat & Kiblat is a Religion & Lifestyle app. Your location data is used exclusively on your device to calculate prayer times and qibla direction — it is never transmitted to any server. We prioritize user privacy and comply with Google Play Developer policies, GDPR, and CCPA.

1. Information Collection and Use

For a better experience while using our application, we may require certain information:

  • Device location (GPS/network-based) — used only on-device to calculate accurate prayer times and qibla direction, never transmitted to any external server
  • Device identifiers (for AdMob ad delivery — collected by Google, not by us)
  • Prayer schedule and notification preferences (stored locally on device)

Location Privacy Guarantee: Your location data is processed entirely on your device using offline Islamic prayer time algorithms. No location coordinates are ever sent to our servers or any third party (other than AdMob's standard ad request, which does not include precise location).

2. Device Permissions

This application requests the following device permissions:

  • ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION / ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION: To determine your geographic location for calculating prayer times and qibla direction accurately. This data stays on your device only.
  • SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM / USE_EXACT_ALARM: To deliver adzan (prayer call) notifications precisely at prayer times.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS: To display prayer time and adzan reminder notifications.
  • VIBRATE: To vibrate the device as part of adzan notifications.
  • INTERNET / ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Required for Google AdMob SDK to display advertisements.

3. Third-Party Services and Advertisements

The app uses third-party services that may collect information used to identify you:

  • Google Play Services: Used for core Android platform features. (Google Privacy Policy)
  • Google AdMob: Used for advertising monetization (Adaptive Banner). AdMob may use device advertising identifiers to deliver relevant ads. (AdMob Privacy & Terms)
  • Google User Messaging Platform (UMP): Used to gather user consent under GDPR/CCPA regulations.

4. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

This application is not directly targeted at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13.

5. California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA)

Under the CCPA, California residents have the right to:

  • Know what categories of personal information are collected.
  • Request deletion of personal data collected by third-party SDKs.
  • Opt-out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal data for targeted advertising through device settings or Google UMP consent forms.

6. General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR)

For users located in the European Economic Area (EEA) and the UK:

  • We integrate Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP SDK) to obtain consent before loading personalized advertisements.
  • You may change or withdraw your consent at any time via in-app settings or your device advertising identifier reset options.

7. Security

We value your trust in providing us your information. Since our application operates locally on your device and processes location data entirely on-device, your personal data remains protected within the Android application sandbox.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

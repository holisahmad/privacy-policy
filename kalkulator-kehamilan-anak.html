Summary: Kalkulator Kehamilan & Anak is a Health & Parenting Tools app. This service is provided by Nusantara Mobile Studio and is intended for use as is. We prioritize user privacy — all personal health data stays strictly on your device and is never transmitted to any server.

1. Information Collection and Use

For a better experience while using our application, we may require certain information:

  • Device identifiers (for AdMob ad delivery & frequency capping — collected by Google, not by us)
  • Pregnancy & child development data entered by the user (stored purely locally on your device, never transmitted to any server)
  • Reminder schedule preferences (stored locally on device)

Offline-First & Local Storage: All user pregnancy data, HPL calculations, child growth records, and reminder settings are stored strictly locally on your device (using Room SQLite Database and SharedPreferences). We do not operate any external server that stores your personal health data.

2. Device Permissions

This application requests the following device permissions:

  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS: To send pregnancy and child care reminder notifications.
  • SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM / USE_EXACT_ALARM: To deliver reminders at the exact scheduled time.
  • RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED: To restore reminder schedules after device restart.
  • INTERNET / ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Required for Google AdMob SDK to display advertisements.

3. Third-Party Services and Advertisements

The app uses third-party services that may collect information used to identify you:

  • Google Play Services: Used for core Android platform features. (Google Privacy Policy)
  • Google AdMob: Used for advertising monetization (Adaptive Banner, Interstitial). AdMob may use device advertising identifiers (such as Google Advertising ID) to deliver relevant ads. (AdMob Privacy & Terms)
  • Google User Messaging Platform (UMP): Used to gather user consent under GDPR/CCPA regulations.

4. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

This application (Kalkulator Kehamilan & Anak) is intended for adult users (parents and expecting mothers) and is not directly targeted at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13.

5. California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA)

Under the CCPA, California residents have the right to:

  • Know what categories of personal information are collected.
  • Request deletion of personal data collected by third-party SDKs.
  • Opt-out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal data for targeted advertising through device settings or Google UMP consent forms.

6. General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR)

For users located in the European Economic Area (EEA) and the UK:

  • We integrate Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP SDK) to obtain consent before loading personalized advertisements.
  • You may change or withdraw your consent at any time via in-app settings or your device advertising identifier reset options.

7. Security

We value your trust in providing us your information. Since our application operates locally on your device, your user data remains protected within the Android application sandbox. However, please remember that no method of electronic storage is 100% secure.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy
